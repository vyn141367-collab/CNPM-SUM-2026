\section{Product Overview }
The AI-powered Film Photography Contest Management Platform is a web and mobile application designed to support the organization and management of film photography contests. The platform provides a centralized environment where administrators, contest organizers, judges, and participants can perform contest-related activities efficiently.
The system supports the entire contest lifecycle, including contest creation, participant registration, photograph submission, judging management, result publication, and digital archiving. Participants can upload scanned film photographs together with technical information such as film stock, camera, lens, ISO, film format, frame number, shooting location, developing laboratory, and scanning specifications.
To improve contest management, the platform integrates Artificial Intelligence (AI) for submission verification and analysis. AI technologies help detect duplicate or highly similar photographs, identify AI-generated images, automatically categorize photographs, generate image keywords, and produce statistical reports for contest organizers.
By integrating contest management, judging workflows, AI-assisted verification, and digital asset management into a single platform, the system improves management efficiency, enhances transparency throughout the judging process, and preserves valuable film photography records for future contests, exhibitions, and educational activities.
