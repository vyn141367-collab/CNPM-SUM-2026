\section{User Requirements}
This section describes the requirements of the main users who interact with the AI-powered Film Photography Contest Management Platform. The system shall provide the necessary functions to support contest management, photograph submission, judging activities, and AI-assisted verification.
\begin{itemize}
\item Participants shall be able to create an account, log in securely, and manage their personal profiles.
\item Participants shall be able to register for photography contests and submit photographs together with the required metadata before the submission deadline.
\item Participants shall be able to track the status of their submissions, receive feedback, and view contest results after publication.
\item Judges shall be able to access assigned submissions, evaluate photographs according to the judging criteria, and submit scores and comments.
\item Contest Organizers shall be able to create contests, configure contest rules, verify submissions, assign judges, and publish contest results.
\item Administrators shall be able to manage user accounts, configure system settings, and monitor overall system activities.
\item The system shall integrate AI services to detect duplicate photographs, identify AI-generated images, and support image categorization for contest management.
\end{itemize}
